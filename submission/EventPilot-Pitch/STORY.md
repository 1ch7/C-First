# EventPilot pitch story

Audience: Dormathon Track 2 judges reviewing C-First, Taylor's University. Goal: demonstrate a genuine sourced, parallel, validated planning workflow and clear execution boundaries, not overclaim LLM autonomy. Ten English pages. Tone: evidence-led, practical, approachable, transparent. Must cover identity, user goal, DAG, weather re-plan, budget, sourced outreach, browser verification and limitations. No invented public URL, bookings, contacts, prices, availability or model calls.

No contents/catalogue page or chapter-divider contract is needed for this short pitch. Heroes: pages 1, 5 and 10 (30%), separated by supporting pages. At least six asymmetric pages. No more than one equal-card row. Rhythms: peak, valley, transition, valley, peak, valley, transition, valley, transition, peak.
